## Notes from the consolidator

Consolidated 4150 parent labels into 125 canonical mechanism classes. Classes are ordered roughly by population (data/measurement/model classes c01-c04 are most populated; specialised mechanism classes follow). A small number of labels (very long tail mid-tail or genuinely narrow specialised mechanisms) were assigned 'none' where no canonical class fit cleanly. Boundary cases that often confuse: (a) capacity/sizing (c17) vs network capacity (c15) — c15 is electrical-network specific; (b) tariff design (c30) vs price signal absent/distorted (c25) — c30 covers structurally-designed tariffs producing perverse outcomes, c25 covers absence/distortion of signal; (c) regulatory framework gap (c34) vs misfit (c35) — c34 = no rule exists, c35 = rule exists but assumes wrong context; (d) stakeholder engagement (c55) vs trust (c56) vs alignment divergence (c113) — c55 = process/timing, c56 = perception/trust, c113 = substantive divergence of interests; (e) coordination (c44) vs responsibility gaps (c45) — c44 = multi-party friction, c45 = no party owns the action.
