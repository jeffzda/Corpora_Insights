# ANAO ↔ ARENA parent overlap audit

**Cost:** $0.46, 187s

## Verdict

Roughly 9 mechanisms map cleanly between the taxonomies — data quality, sample bias, IT systems, cybersecurity, workforce capacity, multi-party coordination, policy uncertainty, equity exclusion, and contract specification. Another ~15 ANAO parents partially overlap ARENA at higher abstraction levels (planning, governance/responsibility, regulatory enforcement, stakeholder engagement, capability erosion). The two taxonomies are substantially independent rather than nested. ANAO's taxonomy is dominated by accountability-and-compliance machinery: upward reporting integrity, statutory authority alignment, transaction controls, eligibility verification, complaints handling, oversight bodies, machinery-of-government transitions, deferral-normalisation — all distinctively Westminster-government failure modes. ARENA's taxonomy is dominated by physical-engineering, power-system, and project-finance mechanisms (sensors, materials, grid dynamics, bankability, supply chains, social licence) that ANAO's administrative corpus simply does not surface. ANAO is therefore not a general programme-failure superset with ARENA's clean-energy specifics carved off; it is itself a specialised vocabulary — for accountability and administrative compliance — that omits much engineering, market, and behavioural mechanism space. The shared core (~10–15 parents) sits in the cross-cutting area: data, IT/security, workforce, coordination, contracting, equity, policy uncertainty.
