# <a id="_Toc151122443"></a>Introduction

Thank you for your interest in the Australian Renewable Energy Agency \(ARENA\)\.

Applying for and maintaining a grant with ARENA is managed via our ARENANet platform\. To gain access to ARENANet, you are required to create a user account\.

To ensure your security and ours, we secure the login process with multi\-factor authentication \(MFA\)\. On the second occasion you log in to the ARENANet platform, you will be requested to select your preferred method of authentication\.

Once you have registered, there are further user guides available to you for:

- [Applying for a grant](https://arenalive.my.salesforce.com/sfc/p/Mn0000000ONJ/a/Mn0000009D65/R7PwTqyd4TX7xYYZ1Ypt3RS7AI5yJmfMOQaJ5BasC8Q) 
- [Maintaining a grant](https://arenalive.my.salesforce.com/sfc/p/Mn0000000ONJ/a/Mn0000009D9J/lZWiEKe6ByXb0rSh5s7f2OhXNfKAnZKWoRr39fenBNE)\.

# <a id="_Toc151122445"></a>Setting up Multi\-Factor Authentication \(MFA\)

Once you have created your ARENANet account and you log in for a second time, you will be required to select a method of multi\-factor authentication\.

1. Follow this link [ARENANet](https://arenanet.arena.gov.au/s) 
2. Enter your username and password\. Click on ‘Log in’

- Universal Second Factor or FIDO2 involves the usage of either the inbuilt biometric authentication on your device or a plug\-in device as shown below\.
