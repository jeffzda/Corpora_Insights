# Lessons Learnt Report Template 

__*Drafting notes: *__

- __*Aim of the report*__*: to share challenges and lessons learnt from the Project during the reporting period that could be useful for the broader industry, including other individuals / organisations undertaking similar projects or considering similar opportunities\.*
- __*Things to keep in mind*__* when writing this Report:*
	- *This report will be made public\.*
	- *Please use your organisation’s branding for the report\.*
	- *Please use plain English, minimise jargon and define technical terms\.*
	- *Please consult *[__*ARENA's Report Writing Tips and Guidelines*__](https://arena.gov.au/assets/2020/03/arena-report-writing-guidelines.pdf)<a id="footnote-ref-2"></a>[\[1\]](#footnote-2)* document, which outlines all mandatory inclusions for public reports\.*
	- *Remember to number all pages, and if your report is over 6 pages long, please include a *__*hyperlinked table of contents*__* on its own separate page\.*
- *Please include a*__* cover page*__* with the following information: *
	- *Your organisation name, contact email, logo and website \(if applicable\) *
	- *The name of your Project*
	- *The title of your Report, edition and reporting period \(i\.e\., Lessons Learnt Report No\. 3 \(July – December 2022\)*
	- *The name of the author*
	- *The date of submission *
	- *An acknowledgement and disclaimer that the Project received funding from ARENA \(as described in your funding agreement\)\. For example:*

This Project received funding from the Australian Renewable Energy Agency \(ARENA\) as part of ARENA’s Advancing Renewables Program \(*or as per your executed Funding Agreement\)\.*
