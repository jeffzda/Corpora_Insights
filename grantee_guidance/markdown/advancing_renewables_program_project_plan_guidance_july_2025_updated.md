Advancing Renewables Program 
Project Plan Guidance 
A ‘Project Plan’ is a mandatory application document under the Advancing Renewables Program (ARP). It forms 
the basis of an application’s merit assessment along with the ARENANet online submission form and other 
attachments. It should be stand-alone and comprehensive, referencing attachments as necessary.  
This document is intended to guide applicants in developing a Project Plan at the Expression of Interest (EOI) and 
Full Application stages. Please note that: 
• 
Guidance is indicative only and should be read in conjunction with the ARP Guidelines, General Funding 
Strategy, Investment plan, and any Funding Announcements as published on the Program webpage.  
• 
It is the Applicant's responsibility to structure a Project Plan to best support an application. 
• 
Applicant’s may use their own templates and styles and include additional content to that outlined below.  
• 
A Project Plan submitted at the Full Application stage may build on the EOI submission, noting that Full 
Applications require significantly more detail that EOIs.  
Note that Applicants are required to contact ARENA via the ARENANet Funding Advice page to discuss their 
potential project prior to submitting an application. 
Table 1 provides an outline of the key information required by the Program Guidelines that should be included in a 
Project Plan. This should be considered a guide only (not a template) and may be modified as desired to best 
represent a specific Project. 
The symbols below indicate the threshold of information that is required at the EOI and Full Application stages.

Capability and Capacity (Merit Criterion B) 
Capability 
• 
Details of Applicant and project partners, including roles, responsibilities, 
experience, and evidence of support for the project (such as letters of 
support, term sheets).  
• 
Experience should cover all relevant skill sets to undertake the project, 
including (but not limited to) commercial, technical, project management, 
legal, knowledge sharing, community engagement, regulatory, WHS.  
 
 
Capacity 
• 
Details of Applicant and project partners resourcing, including personnel, 
physical resources and access to supply chains, facilities, equipment, and 
infrastructure. 
• 
This should include the names and experience of key personnel and 
management, and the individual and total FTE allocated to the project. 
 
 
Governance 
• 
Project governance arrangements and the nature and status of any 
required agreements with project partners.  
 
 
Project Design and Methodology (Merit Criterion C) 
Delivery Methodology 
• 
A well-articulated plan for delivering the project, including the objectives 
and success criteria, workstreams, milestones, and any stage gate(s) or 
decision points.  
 
 
Technical Design 
• 
Details of technical design, including details of proposed hardware and 
software. 
• 
Explanation of current TRL and CRI, and the improvement expected to be 
achieved through the project.  
• 
Relevant Intellectual Property (IP) creation and rights 
 
 
Schedule 
Detailed project timeline (Gantt chart or similar), including: 
• 
Workstreams (with key activities and dependencies).  
• 
Proposed project milestones for ARENA grant funding payments.  
• 
Any stages and stage gates.  
• 
Details and evidence to support assumptions on timings.  
 
 
Procurement 
• 
Details and status of procurement activities, including land and/or site 
access, equipment, and contractors.  
 
 
Approvals 
• 
Details of all required approvals process(es) / licenses / permits to 
undertake the project, including planning, regulatory, environmental, 
heritage and First Nations. 
 
 
Risk 
• 
Summary of the key risks and approach to management and mitigation. 
Detailed risk information can be included in the Risk Management Plan. 
 
 
Community 
• 
Details of community engagement, including identification of stakeholders 
and engagement activities.  
• 
Where the project has the potential to impact community stakeholders 
(such as local community, First Nations groups), a separate Community 
Consultation Plan should be developed). 
 
 
Funding Agreement 
Compliance 
• 
A table stating any general conditions of the draft Funding Agreement that  
you do not accept, accept in part or do not consider to be applicable.

Financial Capacity 
• 
Details of applicant and project partner financial capacity and co-funding 
commitment to deliver the project, including the sources of required 
funding and any restrictions on accessing those funds (such as applicant 
investment committee approval, achieving financial close). 
 
 
ARENA grant 
justification 
• 
Justification of the requested ARENA grant amount that demonstrates the 
minimum amount of funding required for the project to proceed. 
• 
This should be supported with details of the Applicant’s (and any funding 
partners) investment criteria, such as required hurdle rate. 
• 
The Financial Model attachment must reflect this justification (such as by 
showing the project returns / NPV / payback time with and without the 
ARENA grant).   
 
 
Project budget 
• 
Project budget with detailed cost breakdown, sources and uses of funds 
(cash and in-kind).  
• 
The budget may be incorporated into the Financial Model attachment, and 
note that the budget covers only the period of the ARENA project, while 
the financial model should cover the life of the asset for which returns are 
assessed over. 
• 
Evidence for the budget assumptions, such as quotes / orders, industry 
benchmarking and analysis, prior experience.  
• 
Includes the budget allocation for Knowledge Sharing. 
 
 
Contingency 
• 
Details of the budget contingency allowed, including substantiation of the 
appropriateness relative to state of project definition and risk.  
• 
Details of approach to managing cost overruns (above the budget and 
contingency amounts).  
 
 
Knowledge Sharing (Merit Criterion E) 
Knowledge sharing  
• 
Proposed knowledge sharing audience, activities and outputs (e.g. reports, 
events, datasets, etc). 
• 
Value of the knowledge generated (why is it important and aligned with 
Program Objectives) 
• 
A Knowledge Sharing Plan is required as part of Funding Agreements, and 
Applicants may develop this for the application.
